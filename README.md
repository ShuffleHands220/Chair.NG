Chair.NG! a fork of ChairPlay, an apple carplay receiver for Windows.

Setup is simple. go grab Chairplay from https://github.com/NumberOneBot/ChairPlay/releases and install it to your PC
Once you're done with that head over to C:\Users\yourusername\ChairPlay\dist\ChairPlay and drop in "chairplay-beamng.cmd. I recommend creating a shortcut for your desktop while you're here.

Next up, installing the beam.ng mod itself. launch beam.ng and hit manage user folder, then open user folder, head on into the mods folder and drop in the chairplay_bridge.zip. dont even bother unzipping it, beam.ng mods are in a .zip format.

next up is configuring ChairPlay. connect your iPhone like you normally would, then go to settings by hitting the chairplay app on the carplay homescreen. on the sidebar you'll see AltScreen, thats your gauges. enable them, and I recommend setting the content to HUD, which will show currently playing songs from your iPhone. now head over to GPS, and enable spoofing, send vehicle speed, send the heading, send satellites in view, all that good stuff. dont enable drive mode, the gauges will show when beam.ng connects. remember to set MPH/KPH depending on your region.

That should be everything you need to get this up and running. throw the gauge window onto a separate display or a tablet with the Spacedesk app.
